Questions before you pack?

Here are the things travellers ask us most. Still wondering about something? Talk to us.

How do I book a trip?+

Fill out the booking form and pay the required ₹1,000 advance per person to confirm your seat.

Are solo travellers welcome?+

Absolutely. Most Compaspanion travellers join trips solo and meet like-minded people along the way.

Are pets allowed?+

Yes, on selected pet-friendly trips. Please tell us before booking so appropriate arrangements can be considered.

Is travel insurance included?+

No. Travellers are encouraged to arrange their own travel insurance.
