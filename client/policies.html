Terms & booking

Confirming your seat

A booking is confirmed once the required advance payment has been received.

₹1,000 advance per personThe remaining balance must be paid before or at the beginning of the trip, as instructed by Compaspanion.

Bookings are non-transferable unless specifically approved by Compaspanion. Travellers must carry a valid government-issued photo ID and share relevant medical conditions, allergies, or special requirements before the trip.

Respect for fellow travellers, local communities, local culture, and nature is essential. Harassment, discrimination, violence, disruptive intoxication, or illegal activity may result in immediate removal from the trip without a refund.

Cancellation policy

Plans change. Here's what to know.

15+ daysA full refund of the advance is available according to applicable policy terms.
7–14 daysA partial refund may be considered.
Less than 7 daysNo refund applies for late cancellation or no-show.

If Compaspanion cancels a trip because of unavoidable circumstances, travellers will receive either a full refund or the option to transfer their booking to another trip.

Privacy policy

Your information. Your trust.

Information collected through the booking form is used for trip planning, communication, emergency coordination, and booking management. Traveller contact details are kept secure.

Compaspanion does not sell or share personal information with third parties unless required by law or necessary to complete a booking with trusted travel partners.

Health & safety

Your safety matters.

Weather conditions, road closures, natural disasters, government restrictions, travel delays, and other unforeseen circumstances can be outside the organiser's control. Travellers are responsible for their personal belongings throughout the trip.

Adventure activities are undertaken at the traveller's own discretion and risk. Honest medical information helps us plan a safer experience for everyone.
